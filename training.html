Complete Curriculum

The 12 Core Training Modules

Structured sequentially to take you from foundational concepts to advanced brand portfolio management.

Module 01

Amazon FBA Basics

Understanding the Amazon business model, fulfillment mechanics, fee breakdowns, referral structures, and business fundamentals.

Module 02

Product Research & Validation

Data-backed opportunity hunting, trend validation, keyword volume, competition saturation checks, and net profit margins.

Module 03

Supplier Sourcing & Negotiation

Manufacturer vetting on Alibaba, request for quotation (RFQ) procedures, sample testing, DDP shipping terms, and contracts.

Module 04

Branding & Packaging

Brand naming, USPTO/UKIPO trademark basics, packaging dieline design, UPC/GS1 barcode acquisition, and Amazon Brand Registry.

Module 05

Seller Central Setup

Navigating the Seller Central portal, user permissions, charge/deposit methods, entity verification, and settings configuration.

Module 06

Listing SEO & Copywriting

Keyword research, index optimization, crafting benefit-rich titles, persuasive bullet points, and search term placement.

Module 07

Graphics & A+ Content

Planning photography, rendering infographics, lifestyle imagery, Brand Story design, and Enhanced Brand Content (EBC).

Module 08

FBA Shipment & Logistics

Creating inbound shipping plans, FNSKU carton labels, booking freight forwarders, and handling customs clearance.

Module 09

PPC Advertising Mastery

Auto, Broad, Phrase, and Exact campaign setups, product targeting, bid strategies, placement multipliers, and ACOS management.

Module 10

Inventory & Profit Auditing

Restock forecasting, managing lead times, monitoring IPI storage limits, preventing stockouts, and SKU profitability analysis.

Module 11

Account Health & Security

Monitoring policy compliance, avoiding intellectual property strikes, resolving customer defects, and managing performance notifications.

Module 12

Launch & Scaling

30-day post-launch velocity strategy, review generation compliance, expanding into UK, UAE, and Canada, and exit valuation.
